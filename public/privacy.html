Privacy policy

YouNeeK Pro Radar · Updated November 26, 2026

YouNeeK Pro Radar shows radar and severe-weather alerts for a place you choose or for your current location. This policy covers the website and the iOS and Android apps.

Precise location is used only to center the map, load forecasts, and decide whether a National Weather Service warning or watch covers you so the I’m Safe and I Need Help drafts can appear. On iOS the app asks for Location While Using the App. It does not ask for Always location and it does not track you in the background.

When a forecast or alert is loaded, the latitude and longitude are sent to our Cloudflare Worker at youneek-pro-radarynk222.youneekartifacts.workers.dev. That Worker asks Apple WeatherKit and the National Weather Service for data for that point. The app also sends the same coordinates directly to Open-Meteo for forecast fields WeatherKit does not fill. City and ZIP searches are sent to those geocoders so we can turn the name into coordinates. Radar pictures come from RainViewer and Iowa State University's Mesonet; the map view those tile requests reveal is the area on screen, not a saved location profile. Hurricane and wildfire feeds are loaded without your coordinates.

We do not run analytics, advertising, or attribution SDKs. We do not use Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, the advertising identifier, or any third-party tracker. The Worker does not keep an account for you. It does not store your precise location. The only thing it keeps is the warning-alert registration described next, if you turn warning alerts on.

Warning alerts (iOS and Android apps): if you allow notifications, the app sends our Worker this phone's push notification token and your location rounded to about 11 km (0.1°), never your exact location. The Worker looks up the National Weather Service forecast zone and county for that rounded point and stores only the token, those zone codes, and the rounded point in Cloudflare Workers KV. The record is deleted automatically after 30 days without the app being opened, or right away if Apple or Google report the token is no longer valid. Every two minutes the Worker checks the National Weather Service for new Tornado, Severe Thunderstorm, and Flash Flood Warnings and sends a notification through Apple Push Notification service or Firebase Cloud Messaging to phones in the warned area. Apple and Google deliver the notification; they see the token and the warning text. The registration is not linked to your name, contacts, or any account, and it is never used for tracking or advertising. Turn notifications off in the phone's Settings, or delete the app, to stop alerts.

Safety contacts stay on this device (iOS Preferences, or the browser's local storage on the website). They are not uploaded. Tapping I’m Safe or I Need Help opens a message you can read. It names the alert and your nearby town, and includes one map link to where you are and your GPS coordinates. The message is sent only if you tap Send in Messages or in the share sheet. The app does not send it for you and does not resend it.

WeatherKit credentials stay on the Cloudflare Worker. They are not in the app.

You can clear contacts, saved places, and settings in the app with Clear all app data, or by deleting the app. You can turn location and notifications off in the phone's Settings.
